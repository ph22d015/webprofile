**About the numbers.** The default PCM indices (amorphous 3.9 − 0.05i, crystalline 6.1 − 0.8i) are illustrative, rounded values of the size typically reported for Ge₂Sb₂Te₅ near 1550 nm — not measured data. Replace them with your own ellipsometry fits. The substrate is semi-infinite, so T is the power entering the substrate (no back-surface reflection), and A is the power absorbed in the film (1 − R − T).
