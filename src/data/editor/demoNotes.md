**Model and units.** Films are coherent and isotropic, with semi-infinite incident and substrate media. Enter wavelengths and thicknesses in nm and passive optical constants as *n*, *k* ≥ 0. The starting film has illustrative optical constants. Tabulated values are linearly interpolated, with endpoint values held outside the table.

This implementation follows [OptiStack Solver](https://github.com/ph22d015/Optistack_Solver). It uses real-index Snell angles at oblique incidence and treats any total-internal-reflection interface as fully reflecting. Use a transparent incident medium for interpreting *A* = 1 − *R* − *T* as film absorption. Strongly absorbing oblique stacks and evanescent coupling require a complex-angle model. *T* is power entering the substrate.
